03 — featured work

PharmaCare — Pharmacy & Lab SaaS

A production platform I designed and built end-to-end — now live for real pharmacies.

PharmaCare

● Live in production Sole architect & engineer

A bilingual (Somali / English), multi-tenant SaaS that runs a pharmacy and its laboratory from one screen — sales, inventory, credit, lab, and reporting. Built cloud-first on Firebase with a dual-mode data layer, it stays fast, syncs in real time, and keeps working offline.

  • Point of Sale — barcode scanning, discounts, mobile money, thermal receipts
  • Inventory with batch, expiry & reorder alerts + write-off log
  • Laboratory module — ~115 tests, panels, printable reports
  • Credit (deyn) ledger, shift-based cash close, returns/refunds
  • Reports & dashboards — profit margin, printable Z-reports
  • Role-based dashboards + super-admin platform with billing
