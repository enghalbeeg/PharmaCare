Wax kasta oo farmashigaagu u baahan yahay

Hal nidaam oo ka shaqeeya farmashi iyo shaybaadh isku mar — telefoon, tablet ama kombuyuutar.

POS — Iib degdeg ah

Barcode scanner, discount, mobile money (EVC/Zaad/Sahal/eDahab), rasiidh la daabaco.

Bakhaar — Inventory

Batch, expiry, barcode, reorder level, stock write-off + digniino automatic ah.

Deyn — Credit

Diiwaanka deynta macaamiisha, lacag-bixin, iyo taariikh buuxda oo qof kasta.

Shaybaadh — Lab

~115 baadhitaan (dhiig, kaadi, candhuuf…), natiijo, iyo warbixin la daabaco.

Reports & Dashboard

Dakhli, faa'iido, profit margin %, Z-report la daabaco, iyo iib shaqaale kasta.

Returns / Refunds

Soo-celinta alaabta, stock si toos ah dib loogu celiyo, rasiidh refund gaar ah.

Doorar Shaqaale

Cashier / Pharmacist / Admin — mid kasta wixii u gaar ah oo keliya.
